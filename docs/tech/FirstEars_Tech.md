# The First Doll’s Ears — Technical Model

## 2. The Nature of Doll Hearing

Where humans measure *loudness*, dolls measure *phase*.

Each Doll Ear is a tangle of reflective paths, each one slightly longer or shorter, allowing sound to recombine with itself after infinitesimal delays. These differences in delay cause tiny shifts in phase — and it is these shifts, rather than power, that the Dolls perceive as tone and texture.

Thus, what they hear is not the *force* of a sound, but its *shape* — the interference pattern it leaves as it passes through and returns.

### Example Model (Conceptual)

In mathematical terms, if \( s(t) \) is a sound entering the Doll Ear, she perceives something closer to:

$$
H(f) = \sum_i a_i e^{-j 2 \pi f \tau_i}
$$

where each \( a_i \) represents the strength of a reflection, and \( \tau_i \) the delay of that path.  The interference between these delayed versions defines her “spectral sense.”

## 3. The 60 Hz Dilemma

In the ancient ruins of the Mother’s world, there still echoes a 60 Hz hum — a vestigial ghost from the electric walls.  To the Dolls, this hum is not noise but *terrain*.  Their ears tend to align their resonances *between* the harmonics of 60 Hz, as though walking along ridges between valleys of interference.

They can sense its rhythm the way humans sense light flicker — too constant to ignore, too old to understand.
