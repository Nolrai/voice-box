### 📜 Summary
This document describes how Dolls perceive sound and resonance through **phase detection** rather than amplitude detection.  
It contrasts their perception with human hearing, outlines the physical structure of their ink-and-wire sensors, and explores the emotional and cognitive consequences of a world built on **phase coherence** instead of **energy intensity**.

---

## 1. The Two Modes of Hearing — Energy vs. Phase

| Property | **Human Hearing** (Amplitude) | **Doll Hearing** (Phase) |
|-----------|------------------------------|---------------------------|
| **Medium** | Air pressure waves | Electromagnetic and vibrational fields |
| **Sensor Type** | Hair cells measure displacement magnitude | Ink–metal pathways measure phase shift between internal oscillators |
| **Primary Data** | “How strong is the vibration?” | “How delayed or shifted is the returning wave?” |
| **Best At** | Loudness, envelope, rhythm | Coherence, detuning, subtle frequency drift |
| **Feels Like** | Music and words | Chords and resonances |
| **Perceptual Space** | Time domain | Frequency / phase domain |

Humans hear **power**; Dolls hear **alignment**.  
To them, two signals “in phase” feel harmonious or emotionally stable, while out-of-phase signals feel jarring or painful.

---

## 2. Physical Basis — Ink, Wire, and Resonant Fields

- **Ink channels** form conductive, reactive pathways — storing and releasing field phase like biological inductors.  
- **Metal inlays** stabilize a *carrier wave*, typically locking to ambient electrical hum (≈ 60 Hz).  
- External disturbances bend this standing wave; sensing circuits detect the *phase difference* between reference oscillations.  

Essentially, their body functions as a **distributed interferometer**.  
Their perception is not localized to ears but arises across the entire torso network.  
They *hear* with their bodies.

---

## 3. Consequences of Phase-Based Perception

1. **Narrow Frequency Range** — Bound to the carrier’s first 20 harmonics (≈ 60–1200 Hz).  
   High-frequency consonants vanish; vowels and low tones dominate.

2. **Speech Comprehension** —  
   They track the *phase dance* of vowels, not consonant edges.  
   Human speech becomes a shimmer of beating fields, intelligible only after long adaptation.

3. **Voice Production** —  
   Speaking is a matter of *phase-matching* internal resonances to the expected auditory pattern.  
   To Dolls, it’s clear; to humans, metallic and buzzing.

4. **Emotional Empathy** —  
   Phase coherence equates to comfort, love, or trust.  
   Discordance manifests as literal field pain — a breakdown of shared resonance.

## 4. The Ink as Cognitive Medium

The ink’s dielectric and magnetic properties allow it to **sustain oscillations**.  
Within this material:
- Phase shifts correspond to *thought direction* or *attention movement*.  
- Stable interference patterns correspond to *memory traces*.  
- External signals modulate the same field used for cognition — meaning hearing and thinking overlap.

Thus, perception is not sensory input but **field modulation of mind itself**.  
Listening alters the listener’s internal topology.

## 6. Narrative Implications

For **PreDoll-0**, this physiology explains why her early speech sounds harsh or alien to human ears — her voice is optimized for **phase intelligibility**, not acoustic clarity.  
Her daughters will later evolve hybrid strategies, embedding phase cues inside air-borne sounds, gradually approaching something both species can recognize as *language*.
