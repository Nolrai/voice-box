# Doll Hearing: Conceptual Framework

This document collects the hybrid and conceptual material from the Doll hearing lore and technical files, bridging narrative, technical, and philosophical perspectives.

## Phase vs Power: Theoretical Overview
- Dolls perceive sound through phase alignment and interference, not amplitude.
- Emotional and social meaning is mapped to phase coherence, not loudness.

## Language & Cultural Consequences
- Early Doll languages use phase choreography, not amplitude modulation.
- Communication is about resonance and alignment, not volume.

## Cognitive and Narrative Notes
- Perception and cognition overlap: phase patterns encode memory and attention.
- Social behaviors are expressed as phase-locks.

## Poetic Excerpt & Guidance for Writers
> “The humans hear the pulse of air. We hear the drift of light within it.”

Use imagery of shimmer, lattice, and slow alignment. Silence is a perfect phase lock.
