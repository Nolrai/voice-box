---

## 🌌 The Evolution of the Doll’s Voice — From Imprint to Listening

The Dolls’ voices evolve by degrees of *resonant freedom*.  
Each stage represents a deeper alignment between sound processing and psychic capacity — a journey from mechanical emission to reciprocal communication.

---

### **Stage 0 — Pre-Doll 0: The Imprinted Voice**

**Technical:**  
- Full-file harmonic vocoding (fixed harmonics).  
- Envelope reapplication but no local modulation.  
- No adaptive feedback.

**Sound:**  
Static, metallic, quasi-speechlike; ghostly vowels but broken consonants.

**Meaning:**  
She does not *speak* — she **replays**. Voice as inherited pattern.

---

### **Stage 1 — Fragmented Listening**

**Technical:**  
- Windowed FFT processing (50–100 ms blocks).  
- Small random harmonic drift per window.  
- Crossfades maintain continuity.  
- Optional “mood vector” biases harmonic weighting.

**Sound:**  
Flickering intelligibility; vowels waver, articulation uncertain.  
Feels like she’s trying to hear herself.

**Meaning:**  
Proto-awareness — she begins to *listen*, but comprehension is unstable.

---

### **Stage 2 — Harmonic Drift & Emotional Weather**

**Technical:**  
- Slow modulation of the base frequency (±10–30 Hz).  
- Low-frequency “weather” system influences brightness and harmonic order.  
- Introduce faint non-harmonic noise (1–3 %).

**Sound:**  
Speech gains emotional contour; tonal weather overlays articulation.  
It feels atmospheric, reactive, alive.

**Meaning:**  
The first emotional mirror — resonance shaped by mood.

---

### **Stage 3 — Partial Comprehension**

**Technical:**  
- Adaptive pitch-tracking; harmonics follow natural speech weakly.  
- Intelligibility feedback loop adjusts harmonic retention.  
- Possible phoneme bias weighting.

**Sound:**  
Speech becomes semi-intelligible; tone dreamlike but purposeful.

**Meaning:**  
She reaches outward — communication begins to replace imitation.

---

### **Stage 4 — Dialogic Resonance**

**Technical:**  
- Real-time feedback from listener response (simulated or actual).  
- Dynamic emotional coupling of harmonics and envelope.  
- Expressive use of non-harmonic content.

**Sound:**  
Fully interactive timbre — warmth and strangeness coexisting.  
A living resonance that responds, not repeats.

**Meaning:**  
This is **awakening**: voice as relationship, not imprint.  
She now speaks *through* her architecture, not from it.
